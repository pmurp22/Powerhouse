You can change these in Powerhouse itself: open it and go to **Stats** (Tools → Add-ons →
Powerhouse → Config takes you there). The one exception is **deck_panel_compact**, which is
the More/Less button on the deck list card.

- **daily_goal**: cards per day that keep your streak going and pay the knowledge dividend. A day where you finish every due review also counts for the streak.
- **hud**: show the small ATP pill on the review screen.
- **hud_position**: `bar` (in the bottom bar next to More, never covers a card), or a card corner: `top-right`, `top-left`, `bottom-left`.
- **hud_toasts**: let the HUD show pop-ups while you review: trophies, levels, quests, vesicles and fair-play notes.
- **deck_panel**: show the Powerhouse card under your deck list.
- **deck_panel_compact**: start that card in its smaller form.
- **toolbar_button**: add a Powerhouse button to the top bar (restart Anki after changing).
- **sounds**: small sounds in the Powerhouse window. The review screen never plays sounds.
- **reduce_motion**: turn off animations.
